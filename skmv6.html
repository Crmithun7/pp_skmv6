1. Information We Collect

SKMV6 collects only the information necessary to provide employee attendance management and identity verification services.

The information we may collect includes:

  • Employee ID
  • Employee Name
  • Company Code
  • Facial Biometric Data (Face Embeddings)
  • Facial Images Captured During Registration and Verification
  • Face Verification Results
  • Current GPS Location During Attendance Verification
  • Attendance Records
  • Attendance Date and Time

We do not access personal files, contacts, SMS messages, installed applications, or personal photos stored on your device.

2. Facial Recognition and Biometric Data

SKMV6 uses facial recognition technology exclusively for employee identity verification and attendance authentication. Facial biometric information is treated as sensitive personal data.

2.1 Facial Data Collected

The application processes the following facial information:

  • Facial Images: Images captured through the device's front camera during employee face registration and attendance verification.
  • Face Embeddings: Numerical biometric representations generated from facial features. These templates are used to compare and verify an employee's identity.
  • Verification Results: The results of facial matching used to determine whether the person attempting to record attendance matches the registered employee.

2.2 Purpose of Facial Data Collection

Facial data is collected and processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • Employee identity verification.
  • Employee attendance check-in and check-out.
  • Preventing fraudulent or unauthorized attendance.
  • Matching an employee against their registered facial template.
  • Maintaining the integrity and security of attendance records.

Facial biometric data is not used for advertising, marketing, commercial profiling, or any purpose unrelated to employee attendance management.

2.3 Facial Registration Process

  • The employee registers their face through the application's front camera.
  • The application processes the captured facial image and generates a numerical biometric template (face embedding).
  • The generated face embedding is stored in the attendance system for future identity verification.
  • Facial registration is locked after successful registration.
  • An employee cannot independently modify or re-register their facial biometric information unless authorized by the company administrator.

2.4 Facial Verification Process

During attendance verification, the application captures a facial image using the front camera and processes the facial features for comparison against the employee's registered biometric template.

The matching result is used exclusively to authenticate the employee and process the attendance transaction.

3. Camera Permission

SKMV6 requests front camera access only when the employee performs an attendance-related operation.

The camera is used for:

  • Employee facial registration.
  • Employee identity verification.
  • Attendance check-in and check-out authentication.
  • Preventing unauthorized attendance submissions.

The camera is not continuously accessed or used for background surveillance.

4. Location Permission

SKMV6 accesses the device's current GPS location when required for attendance verification.

Location information is used to:

  • Verify employee presence within authorized attendance zones.
  • Prevent attendance fraud.
  • Record the employee's attendance location.

The application does not continuously track employee location in the background.

Collected information is used exclusively for:

  • Employee identity verification.

  • Attendance reporting and record maintenance.

Information collected through SKMV6 may be accessible to the following authorized parties:

  • Employer / Organization: The employee's organization for legitimate attendance and workforce management purposes.
  • Authorized Company Administrators: For employee management, attendance administration, and authorized identity verification operations.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Employee facial biometric information is retained only for as long as the employee is actively associated with the organization and requires attendance verification services, subject to applicable legal and organizational requirements.

Our data retention and deletion practices are as follows:

  • Active Employees: Registered face embeddings are retained while the employee is actively employed and requires facial identity verification for attendance management.
  • Facial Images: Facial images captured during registration and attendance verification are processed for identity authentication. Their storage and deletion follow the system's configured image-retention policy.
  • Employee Leaving the Company: When an employee resigns, is terminated, or otherwise leaves the organization, the authorized company administrator deactivates the employee's attendance access and deletes the employee's registered facial biometric data (face embeddings) from the active attendance system.
  • Employee Account Deletion: When an employee account is permanently deleted, the associated facial biometric templates are also deleted from the active attendance system.
  • Deletion Requests: Employees may contact their organization or authorized administrator to request deletion of their facial biometric information.
  • Attendance Records: Attendance records may be retained separately for legitimate business, payroll, audit, or legal requirements, even after the employee's facial biometric data has been deleted.

Once facial biometric data is no longer required, it will be deleted from the active system in accordance with our retention and deletion procedures, subject to applicable legal requirements.
