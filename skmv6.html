Privacy Policy

Application: SKMV6

Effective Date: June 30, 2026

SKMV6 ("we", "our", or "the Application") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our employee attendance application.

By using SKMV6, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

SKMV6 only collects information necessary to provide attendance and identity verification services.

  • Employee ID
  • Employee Name
  • Company Code
  • Face Registration Data (Face Embeddings)
  • Face Verification Data
  • Current GPS Location (during attendance verification)
  • Attendance Records
  • Camera images captured during face registration and verification

We do not collect device information such as:

  • Device Model
  • IMEI Number
  • Serial Number
  • Installed Applications
  • Contacts
  • SMS Messages
  • Personal Photos
  • Videos
  • Files stored on your device

2. Face Recognition

SKMV6 uses facial recognition technology solely for employee identity verification and attendance.

During face registration:

  • The employee registers their face once.
  • The application extracts secure facial biometric templates (face embeddings).
  • These biometric templates are securely stored for verification purposes.
  • After successful registration, face registration is locked.
  • Employees cannot modify or re-register their face unless authorized by the company administrator.

Face data is never used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

3. Camera Permission

SKMV6 requests access to the device's front camera only for:

  • Employee face registration
  • Employee identity verification
  • Attendance authentication
  • Preventing fraudulent attendance

The camera is only activated when the employee performs face registration or attendance verification.

4. Location Permission

SKMV6 requests access to your current GPS location only when required for attendance verification.

Location is used to:

  • Verify employee presence within authorized attendance zones
  • Prevent attendance fraud
  • Record attendance location for organizational purposes

SKMV6 does not continuously track your location in the background. Location is accessed only during attendance verification.

5. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only for:

  • Employee identity verification
  • Attendance management
  • Attendance reporting
  • Preventing unauthorized attendance
  • Improving application security

6. Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information.

  • Secure server storage
  • Encrypted HTTPS communication
  • Access control for authorized personnel
  • Database security measures
  • Regular security updates

While we strive to protect your information, no electronic storage system is completely secure.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Your information may only be shared with:

  • Your employer or organization
  • Authorized company administrators
  • Government or legal authorities when required by applicable law

8. Data Retention

We retain employee information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Maintain attendance records
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Meet organizational requirements

When information is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

9. User Rights

Depending on applicable laws, users may have the right to:

  • Access their personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of personal information (subject to company policies and legal obligations)
  • Contact the administrator regarding privacy concerns

10. Children's Privacy

SKMV6 is intended solely for employee attendance management and is not designed for individuals under the age of 18.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will become effective immediately upon publication of the updated Privacy Policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact your organization or the SKMV6 support team.
